Pretend pedalboard

Notes

  • The surface: drag to pan, pinch or ctrl-scroll (or the + / − buttons) to zoom, the wheel to scroll. Long-press a pedal to lift it and drop it on a free cell (the wires follow). Tap a pedal: it would open its settings screen.
  • Tap a + on the surface: the pedals nearest it come first in Input from and Output to (before the + leads the inputs, after it the outputs). Place puts the new pedal in that cell.
  • One empty column and row stay at the right and bottom; when a pedal fills them, Expand surface adds another.
  • Place adds the pedal and its wires to this pretend board, so the next + can pick it as an input or output.
  • For the phone layout, use the browser's phone size: the dialog goes full screen.
