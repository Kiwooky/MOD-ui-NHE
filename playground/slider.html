What to try

  • Tap the track: nothing happens. Only the thumb drags, and it never jumps to your finger.
  • Press and hold the thumb without moving: the ring turns to the theme colour and a magnifier appears. Now drag: it moves 10× finer.
  • Tap the readout and type a value; Enter commits, Escape cancels. Out-of-range values are clamped.
  • Pick a binding: the slider still works (shared, like MIDI) and the binding icon appears next to the settings icon; tap it for the details. Tick "Locked": it goes grey, the icon moves onto the thumb, and a touch shows the orange ring and the details.
  • In the sample panel, Q is shared with MIDI and Pan is locked to CV.
  • "High cut" is logarithmic, "Voices" is stepped (ticks, snapping), "Pan" crosses zero (fill from zero).
  • Long values shrink to fit the readout (try "Low cut" while dragging).
  • Change the theme colour at the top: everything follows the accent token.
