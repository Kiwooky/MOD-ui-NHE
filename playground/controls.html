What to try

  • Change the column height: the Delay stack holds 3 cells at 646, wraps sooner at 400, and fits everything in one column at 900. Group bands and names follow the columns.
  • When the panel is wider than the window, a fade with an arrow shows at the right edge; it goes once you scroll to the end. Tap the arrow to scroll.
  • Tap the system bar's chain cards to "hop" (see the log), tap the tempo to type a new one, tick "synced" to lock it, and simulate an xrun.
  • Shared bindings work normally with the icon next to settings; tap it for the details. Locked ones are grey with the icon on the control; a tap shows the orange ring and the details.
  • Preset list: Save, Rename and Delete only work for a user preset.
